# Human readers

## Overview

The people the wiki is for. They read pages, follow links, ask questions, and point agents at new sources. Most never edit a page.

## Start here (as of 2026-10-04)

- Read in the [[reader-app]], Obsidian, GitHub, or any editor.
- Ask an agent a question and get a cited answer ([[query]]).
- Drop a source into a chat and say "add this to the wiki" ([[ingest]]).
- Check the open questions to see what the wiki doesn't know yet ([[known-unknowns]]).

Readers sit with the [[agent-maintainers]] as the two roles in [[llm-wiki-kit]].
