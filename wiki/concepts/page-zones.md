# Page zones

> Raw: [origin and shape](../../raw/design-notes/2026-10-04-origin-and-shape.md)

## Overview

Long pages follow a fixed reading order so a reader gets what is true now from the first screen. The rule kicks in past 80 lines; shorter pages can skip zones that would be empty.

## Start here (as of 2026-10-04)

| Zone | Holds | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | What it is and what it is not | Two short paragraphs |
| Start here | What is true now, dated | Within the first 40 lines |
| Stable body | Definition, process, model | Updated in place |
| Evidence log | Dated field notes, newest first | Or just raw links |
| Known Unknowns | Open items | 7 bullets |
| See Also | Links | No essay |

Soft size limits per type live in [[shared-schema]]. The [[wiki-linter]] warns past them. A page that keeps outgrowing its limit is usually two pages, or one page plus evidence that belongs in raw.

## Numbers in Start here

Apply the [[tomorrow-morning-test]] before writing a number into Start here.
