# Known unknowns

> Raw: [origin and shape](../../raw/design-notes/2026-10-04-origin-and-shape.md)

## Overview

The wiki tracks what it does not know as carefully as what it does. Uncertainty has four homes, from light to heavy.

## Start here (as of 2026-10-04)

| Layer | Where | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known Unknowns | Last section of any page | What this page's sources don't settle |
| Question | A page in `questions/` with status and horizon | An open thread worth tracking |
| Working claim | `hypothesis` on the question page | The best current answer, one falsifiable sentence |
| Evidence ledger | `evidence.for` and `evidence.against` | Raw paths that move the claim |

## Rules

- A Known Unknowns bullet that keeps growing becomes a question page.
- The hypothesis is the headline; the `## Answer` section holds the cited detail. Change both in the same edit or neither. [raw](../../raw/design-notes/2026-10-04-origin-and-shape.md)
- Evidence points at raw, never at wiki pages. A claim supported only by another wiki page is circular.
- Every [[ingest]] names the questions it touches and re-reads them.
