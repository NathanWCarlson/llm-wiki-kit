# Supersede in place

> Raw: [origin and shape](../../raw/design-notes/2026-10-04-origin-and-shape.md)

## Overview

When direction changes, rewrite the canon page so its present tense is true today. Don't leave the old plan beside the new one. History lives in the [[raw-layer]] and the [[operations-log]].

## Start here (as of 2026-10-04)

1. Rewrite the current paragraph on the canon page.
2. Optionally add one line: "Superseded (YYYY-MM-DD): what changed", with a raw citation.
3. Search the wiki for the old slogan and fix every present-tense hit.

## Fossils

A fossil is an old plan still written in present tense: "next week we will", "currently", "the plan is". Agents re-read fossils as truth. Date every current-state claim ("Status (2026-10-04):") so a stale one at least shows its age. Whether a lint rule could catch fossils is [[q-automate-fossil-check]].
